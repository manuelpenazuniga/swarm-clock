# Pre-registration: uncertainty factors F1 (one-family clock), F3 (family-definition grid), F2 (phrase-block edits)

Committed 2026-11-08 ~23:58 America/Santiago BEFORE any of the real-data statistics below was computed. Design by an analysis agent, who ran simulation-only pilots (50 reps; 30 for the grid) before this commit; pilot outputs are kept in scripts/uncertainty/out_pilot/. No per-family p-value and no real-data statistic for the new configurations had been computed.

**Disclosure for F1 (effect-size-informed, exploratory).** Per-family long-lag rho are already published (Table 2), and a simulation-based estimate (no-clock null SD from star lineages) puts round-status reports near p ~ 0.01 under the per-family test. F1 is therefore labeled effect-size-informed exploratory.

**F1. Per-family long-lag tests.** For each of the four primary families with >= 10 representative pairs at lag >= 6 h: one-sided permutation p-value of rho_f (length strata, all pairs, B = 9,999); Holm across the four at familywise alpha = 0.05. Power: D13 end-to-end machinery with a token clock planted in one family at a time and no-clock stars (rate 3p) in the other three; rates 0.005, 0.01, 0.02, 0.05; 200 replicates; B = 399. Report P(p_f <= 0.0125) (a lower bound on Holm power), pooled-T detection with the other three real families as observed, and the stars' familywise error. Reading: a Holm rejection is reported as long-lag association in that family (clock and exogenous drift not distinguished). A non-rejection excludes a one-family clock at a given rate only where the 95% Wilson lower limit of that power is >= 0.8.

**F3. Family-definition grid (exploratory robustness).** k in {3, 4, 5} x LSH threshold in {0.25, 0.30, 0.35} (0.40 gives the same LSH bands as 0.35). Configurations whose largest family exceeds 20% of blocks are excluded. For each configuration: pooled long-lag T (all pairs and cross-label; length strata; B = 999) over its eligible families, and D13 end-to-end power at 0.01 and 0.05 (100 replicates, planted in that configuration's own multi-day families). All configurations are reported. A configuration shows long-lag association only if p < 0.05/9. Configurations with end-to-end power < 0.5 at both rates are labeled uninformative, whatever their T.

**F2. Phrase-block edit model (exploratory).** D13 design with the per-copy mutation replaced by K ~ Poisson(1.5 p n / 4) block events (block length ~ Geometric, mean 4): 50% replace with a contiguous block from a donor, 25% delete, 25% insert a donor block. Donors: (a) real introductions of the same family; (b) random corpus introductions. Rates 0.01, 0.02, 0.05, 0.10, 0.20; 200 replicates. Detection and recovery are reported against the realized mean parent-child Jaccard distance delta and compared with the token model at matched delta. (Block-edit stars are not implemented in the script and are not run; test size is taken from the token-model stars of D13.)
